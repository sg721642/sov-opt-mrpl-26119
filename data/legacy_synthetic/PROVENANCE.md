## Synthetic nature

All files in this directory were generated programmatically by scripts/make_examples.py.
The "refinery" naming is metaphorical. The cost coefficients (52, 34), volumes (60, 50),
quality bounds (-0.5*x + 1.5*y ≤ 0), and MILP activation cost (120 units) are chosen
for numerical clarity, NOT from any MRPL plant data, industry report, or published study.

## What the baseline results mean

The previously reported objectives (LP=4865, MILP=4985, QP≈5009.5) are correct
numerical solutions to these specific synthetic models. They do NOT demonstrate:
- Solver accuracy on real refinery optimization problems
- Any validated MRPL economics
- Any claimed percentage accuracy against industrial solutions
